Our purpose

To create real opportunities for children and young people through sport, education and environmental sustainability.

We develop leaders who contribute to their communities and build life plans with a positive impact.

One opportunity can change the course of a life. We believe that transforming a territory starts with creating opportunities for the people who live there.

Founded in Bogotá on July 11, 2025

Mission

To create opportunities for children, teenagers and young people across Colombia, with a presence in Bogotá, Vaupés and Chocó, especially for those facing vulnerability, economic hardship or barriers to opportunity. Through sport, education and sustainable development, we strengthen their skills, nurture their talents and support life plans that contribute to the development and transformation of their communities and territories.

Vision

To be a leading organization in Colombia in creating opportunities that transform lives and territories, expanding our impact to regions such as La Guajira, Guaviare, Amazonas and Valle del Cauca. We want more children, teenagers, young people and communities to develop their skills, nurture their talents and build a future with greater opportunities for growth, participation and well-being.

Our story

From a neighborhood pitch to three lines of work

We started with a ball in Engativá and Barrios Unidos. Fifteen years later, sport opened the door to education and to caring for the land.

2010

It all begins

John Cárdenas, a sports graduate from the Universidad Pedagógica Nacional and former Independiente Santa Fe player, begins coaching and mentoring young talent in Engativá, starting with futsal.

2012

Seven Colombia is born

An invitation to international 7-a-side tournaments leads to the first team under the name Seven Colombia. In Brazil and Mexico we saw the rise of a sport on its way to going professional, and the Seven Bogotá F.C. club was formally founded.

2015

Joining the FIF7

After joining the International 7-a-side Football Federation, the club began competing every year in international and national tournaments. Since then, more than 30 young players have gone on to opportunities with professional clubs in Colombia and abroad.

2025

The Foundation is established

On July 11, 2025, Fundación Sevenfold Colombia was legally founded to give the program an institutional structure. We created Seven Vaupés F.C. in Mitú, reached more than 280 children and young people and celebrated titles in Mérida, Mexico.

2026

Three lines on the ground

School kits and shoes in Vaupés; a humanitarian response of more than 15 tonnes for Chocó, where Seven Chocó is born; the launch of our environment line; and the F7 Intercontinental Cup in Rome.

We are a non-profit organization

Fundación Sevenfold Colombia is a non-profit organization committed to social transformation through sport, education and sustainable development.

We work to create opportunities for children, teenagers, young people and communities, especially in territories with limited access to holistic development programs.

Every contribution becomes an opportunity. The resources we receive go toward our social mission, promoting inclusion, talent and the building of life plans.
