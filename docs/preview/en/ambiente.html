Line 03 · Environment and sustainable development

Caring for the land also creates opportunities

Educating 21st-century young people also means awakening their environmental awareness and their commitment to sustainable development.

Why the environment

Inspiring territories, opportunities to transform

We promote a vision of development that recognizes the value of nature, encourages the responsible use of resources and seeks to raise awareness and drive sustainable action that contributes to community well-being and the conservation of territories.

  • Conservation
  • Biodiversity
  • Sustainable development
Our commitment

Small actions, more sustainable territories

Through small actions and community initiatives we have begun contributing to the well-being of communities and their territories, recognizing the importance of protecting ecosystems, conserving biodiversity and fostering a culture of environmental responsibility.

We aim to build spaces that bring together young people, companies, universities and social organizations in conservation, ecological restoration and environmental education activities, especially in territories such as Vaupés and Chocó.

Our programs

Volunteering and environmental services

Three ways to join the conservation and sustainable development of our territories.

01

Corporate environmental volunteering

Volunteer days in priority territories where company employees and partners take part in ecological restoration, planting native species, recovering degraded areas and environmental awareness, alongside local communities.

02

Academic and university volunteering

Partnerships with universities so students, faculty and researchers can contribute on the ground: ecological restoration, environmental education, participatory ecosystem monitoring, applied research and knowledge transfer.

03

Environmental services and advisory

Supply of bio-inputs and agricultural inputs, advisory and support for conservation and restoration projects, training in agroecological practices and logistical support in remote territories such as Vaupés and Chocó.

The income from these services goes toward our social mission.
