Seven Colombia, endorsed by the FIF7

The Colombian 7-a-side Football Association, endorsed by the International 7-a-side Football Federation (FIF7), officially represents Colombia in international competitions of this sport, promoting the growth, visibility and standing of Colombian 7-a-side football around the world.

Where we work

Different territories, one purpose

From the Pacific to the Amazon. Tap the map.

Bogotá

Where it all began: the pitches of the Engativá and Barrios Unidos districts.

Seven Bogotá brings together children and young people from working-class neighborhoods and diverse social and cultural backgrounds, who find in 7-a-side football a space to grow, build character and shape a life plan. Through sport they develop discipline, teamwork and a competitive mindset, and prepare to represent Colombia at national and international tournaments.

120+young people training for free
7age categories

Vaupés

Talent from the Amazon also plays for Colombia.

In Mitú, Vaupés, we support Seven Vaupés F.C., a sports program that opens opportunities for young people from Indigenous communities of the Colombian Amazon. Through 7-a-side football we strengthen their skills, promote teamwork and help new talent reach national and international competitions.

35young people in Seven Vaupés F.C.

Chocó

Our first steps in Colombia's Pacific region.

In August 2026 we traveled to Chocó with humanitarian aid for communities affected by the earthquake. Now we want to take the next step: launching Seven Chocó to discover and develop the talent of local children and young people, with 7-a-side football as a space for learning, new goals and sporting opportunities.
