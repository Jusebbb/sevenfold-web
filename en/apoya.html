Giving also has benefits for you

We are a non-profit organization (ESAL) recognized by Colombia's tax authority (DIAN) under the Special Tax Regime. This means that individuals and companies supporting our mission may be eligible for a tax credit of 25% of the amount donated against their Colombian income tax, subject to legal requirements and limits.

In-kind donations may also qualify for tax benefits, provided they meet the legal requirements for valuation and certification.

FAQ

Before you join

Have another question? Use the form below or write to fundacionsevenfold@gmail.com.

Is the Foundation legally registered?

Yes. Fundación Sevenfold Colombia (FUNSEVEN) was established on July 11, 2025 in Bogotá, with tax ID (NIT) 901.965.467-5, and belongs to Colombia's Special Tax Regime.

Is Seven Colombia officially endorsed?

Yes. Through the Colombian 7-a-side Football Association, Seven Colombia is the country's official representative to the International 7-a-side Football Federation (FIF7), and has competed in its tournaments since 2015.

Will I receive a donation certificate?

Yes. Every donation receives a certificate for tax purposes with Colombia's tax authority (DIAN).

Do in-kind donations also get a certificate?

Yes. Products donated by an individual or a brand are also donations: they receive a certificate and may qualify for the same tax benefit.

Do the young people pay to take part?

No. Access is free for children and young people from the communities we prioritize. That's why your support matters so much.

Can I choose where my contribution goes?

Yes. You can direct it to a territory, an international delegation, school kits or sponsoring a child. We'll confirm it in the proposal.

How do I know my contribution was well used?

We publish an annual management and results report, and we send partners reports with photos of every activity they funded.

Can I donate from outside Colombia?

Yes. Write to us and we'll let you know the most convenient method for your country.
