Who endorses us

Colombian 7-a-side Football Association

The Colombian 7-a-side Football Association, endorsed by the International 7-a-side Football Federation (FIF7), officially represents Colombia in international competitions of this sport, promoting the growth, visibility and standing of Colombian 7-a-side football around the world.

The endorsement

From the neighborhood to the biggest stages of professional 7-a-side football

Every Seven Colombia player competes within the official 7-a-side system. Here's how it works:

  1. International federation

    FIF7

    The world governing body of 7-a-side football: it organizes the World Cup and the Club World Cup.

  2. Official representative in Colombia

    Colombian 7-a-side Football Association

    Seven Colombia: it regulates, promotes and develops 7-a-side football in the country and selects the national team.

  3. Development and competition

    Our clubs

    Seven Bogotá F.C., Seven Vaupés F.C., Gol7 Colombia and Cafeteros Fut Talla Baja. Seven Chocó is on its way.

  4. Social arm · non-profit

    Fundación Sevenfold

    Makes it possible for kids to train for free, stay in school and travel to compete.

For a partner, this means supporting an official program with measurable results.

  • Official tournamentsOur players compete at FIF7 events wearing Colombia's jersey.
  • Measurable resultsInternational titles, young players at professional clubs and an annual report.
  • Legal backingThe Foundation is a non-profit under Colombia's Special Tax Regime and issues donation certificates.

Bogotá · Engativá and Barrios Unidos

Seven Bogotá F.C.

A professional 7-a-side team with international recognition since 2015. It brings together children and young people from working-class neighborhoods and diverse social and cultural backgrounds, who find in 7-a-side football a space to grow, build character and shape a life plan.

  • 120+young people training for free
  • 7age categories
Mitú · Amazon

Seven Vaupés F.C.

Talent from the Amazon also plays for Colombia. In Mitú we support Seven Vaupés F.C., a sports program that opens opportunities for young people from Indigenous communities. Through 7-a-side football we strengthen their skills, promote teamwork and help new talent reach national and international competitions.

  • 35young people in the club
  • 2have already played on international stages

Adapted sport · Inclusion · International representation

Cafeteros Fut Talla Baja

Colombia's national adapted football team for people of short stature: a sports program built by people with achondroplasia, hypochondroplasia and other skeletal dysplasias, which today represents Colombia internationally. It was formally established at the end of 2025 and has already been featured on outlets such as Win Sports and Buen Día Colombia.

  • 24athletes and 6 members of the management and coaching staff
  • 7cities: Bogotá, Medellín, Cali, Barranquilla, Duitama, Pereira and Tunja
  • 2teams at the 1st Sin Límites International Cup, Guatemala 2026

Seven Chocó

In August 2026 we were in Chocó bringing humanitarian aid to the communities affected by the earthquake. Now we want to take a new step: to drive the creation of Seven Chocó to discover and develop the talent of the region's children and young people, giving them in 7-a-side football a space for training, new goals and a future in the sport.
