Our progress

From 2025 to today

The Foundation was legally established in July 2025. This is what we've achieved since then, with every partner, family and volunteer.

2025

A first year to put down roots

We gave institutional structure to a program with more than fifteen years on the pitches of Bogotá. We supported more than 280 children and young people, created Seven Vaupés F.C. in Mitú with 35 young players, and took 69 athletes to represent Colombia in Curitiba (Brazil) and Mérida (Mexico), where the delegation won three titles.

  • 280+children and young people
  • 69athletes for Colombia
  • 3titles in Mérida

Donation

Your donation can change a life

Behind every child there's a dream. You can help make it real.

At Fundación Sevenfold Colombia we believe talent shouldn't be held back by a lack of opportunities. With your support we bring sport, education and hope to children and young people who dream of a better future.

How can you help?

You can donate in two ways:

  • Cash donationsFinancial contributions that fund our young athletes' international competitions. Minimum donation: COP 500,000.
  • In-kind donationsSports facilities, apparel, uniforms, balls, training equipment and other items that support the training and development of our young talent.

Every contribution, big or small, can open the door to new opportunities.

Giving also has benefits for you: we are a non-profit (ESAL) under Colombia's Special Tax Regime, and your donation may qualify for a tax credit of 25% of the amount donated against Colombian income tax, subject to legal requirements and limits.
